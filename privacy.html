PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Your details are not the product.

Astate is a lightweight property-introduction service. People can submit buyer requirements or property offers without uploading photographs.

What we collect

When you submit a form, we collect the property facts and the contact information you choose to provide. The system stores submissions for review and matching. Do not submit government ID numbers, bank details, title deeds or other highly sensitive documents in a text field.

What becomes public

When a contributor taps Post, the real-estate text is published immediately. Phone numbers, email addresses and URLs are stripped from the public description where detected. A separate private record may retain the original submission for administration if storage succeeds. Do not include sensitive data; public posts may be indexed, copied or cached by third parties. Phone numbers, email addresses and URLs are redacted by basic patterns; other personal details typed into a post may remain public, so do not include names or sensitive information you want kept private. Enquiries should be routed through the published reference and @amormagics unless the site owner arranges a separate, consented introduction.

AI and search engines

Published records may be indexed, copied or summarized by search engines and AI tools. We cannot make third-party crawlers forget information they have already collected. Only post details you are comfortable making public.

Matching and verification

Matching is a best-effort comparison of stated location, property type, size and budget, not a recommendation or verification. Listings may be incomplete, outdated or inaccurate. Independently verify ownership, encumbrances, zoning, land use, access, approvals, taxes and all contractual terms with qualified professionals before paying or signing.

Contact and removal requests

For corrections, withdrawal or privacy questions, contact @amormagics or @arcstudio91 on Instagram and include your listing reference if available. Do not post private contact details in a public comment.

Service status

Astate is an introduction and matching interface, not a guarantee of a transaction, valuation, legal status or property availability. No success fee or brokerage terms are implied by this website.
